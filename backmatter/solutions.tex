\chapter{Solutions}
\label{app:solutions}

These are solutions to every problem in the book. They are written to be checked against, not read in place of doing the problem: each gives the key idea, the main steps, and the final numbers, but leaves routine algebra to you. Numerical answers are rounded to the precision the problem's data justify. Where a problem asks you to explain something, the solution gives one good explanation; there are often others.
