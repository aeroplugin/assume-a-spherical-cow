\solchapter{math}

\sol{math}{10} Write the integrand as $e^{f(x)}$ with $f = N\ln x - x$. Find where $f$ peaks and how sharply: what is $f''$ there?

\sol{math}{11} Substitute $u = 1/\epsilon$. What does every derivative of $e^{-1/\epsilon}$ look like in terms of $u$, and what does $e^{-u}$ do to any polynomial as $u\to\infty$?

\sol{math}{12} Five quantities and three dimensions leave two dimensionless groups; one is $\rho gr^2/\sigma$. For a small tap this group is tiny. What can the other group then depend on?

\solchapter{newton}

\sol{newton}{10} Suppose the top $k$ books balance exactly on the edge of book $k+1$. Where is the centre of mass of the top $k+1$ books, measured from that same edge?

\sol{newton}{11} Go to the rotating frame and add the centrifugal potential $-\tfrac12m\Omega^2(R\sin\theta)^2$ to gravity's. Equilibria and their stability come from the first and second derivatives.

\sol{newton}{12} You will get two coupled linear equations for the small components $\omega_1$ and $\omega_3$. Differentiate one, substitute the other, and look at the sign of the coefficient: it is a product of two differences of moments of inertia.

\solchapter{sho}

\sol{sho}{9} Bound $\langle p^2\rangle$ below by $(\Delta p)^2$ and use $\Delta p\ge\hbar/2\Delta x$. Now the energy is a function of $\Delta x$ alone. Minimize it.

\sol{sho}{10} You need $g\sin\phi = \omega^2s$, i.e.\ $s\propto\sin\phi$. Use $\dd x = \cos\phi\,\dd s$ and $\dd y = \sin\phi\,\dd s$, and write everything in terms of $2\phi$.

\sol{sho}{11} At the instant of standing up, the force on the child is radial, so angular momentum about the pivot is conserved. Between such instants, energy is conserved. Chain the two to relate one amplitude to the next.

\solchapter{analytical}

\sol{analytical}{9} The integrand does not depend on $x$, which plays the role of time. What is the analogue of the conserved energy for a Lagrangian with no explicit time dependence?

\sol{analytical}{10} To leading order the fast jitter is $\xi\approx-(a/\ell)\sin\bar\theta\cos\Omega t$. Expand $\sin(\bar\theta+\xi)$ to first order in $\xi$ and average over one period; $\langle\cos^2\Omega t\rangle = \tfrac12$.

\sol{analytical}{11} Each bounce off a wall approaching at speed $u$ adds $2u$ to the ball's speed. How often does the ball hit that wall? Turn this into a differential equation relating $v$ and $\ell$.

\solchapter{waves}

\sol{waves}{10} In shallow water the group velocity equals $\sqrt{gh}$, and the wave's period does not change as it travels. Conserve (energy per area)$\times$(group velocity).

\sol{waves}{11} Follow individual points of the wave profile, each moving at its own local speed. When do the paths of a crest and the trough in front of it cross?

\sol{waves}{12} Energy emitted when the boat was a distance $D$ behind its present position, in all directions $\theta$, lies on a circle. Find that circle's centre and radius, then the tangent line from the boat.
