\solchapter{accel}

\sol{accel}{10} For the second plane, swap the signs of $f_1$ and $f_2$. For the physics, remember that a lens kicks a ray in proportion to its distance from the axis.

\sol{accel}{11} Power is current times energy lost per turn per unit charge, $P = I\,\Delta E/e$. Find the largest allowed $\Delta E$, then solve the synchrotron formula for $\rho$ at $500\un{GeV}$ per beam.

\sol{accel}{12} If plasma electrons are displaced by $x$, Gauss's law gives $E = nex/\epsilon_0$. How far can they be displaced before the wave breaks? Try $x\sim c/\omega_p$.

\solchapter{particle}

\sol{particle}{10} Check the symmetry of each factor --- space, spin, flavour --- under exchange of two quarks. Then ask what $\epsilon_{ijk}$ does when two indices are swapped, and how it transforms under a rotation of the three colours.

\sol{particle}{11} Each water molecule has $10$ protons. For the limit: if the expected number of decays were $\mu$, the chance of seeing none would be $e^{-\mu}$.

\sol{particle}{12} For each condition, imagine it fails and show the asymmetry cannot develop. In equilibrium, what determines a population, and how do particle and antiparticle compare?

\sol{particle}{13} Compute the amplitude $\braket{\nu_e}{\psi(t)} = \cos^2\theta\,e^{-iE_1t/\hbar} + \sin^2\theta\,e^{-iE_2t/\hbar}$ and take its squared magnitude.

\solchapter{strings}

\sol{strings}{9} Rearrange the rigidity formula for $\rho$. For the synchrotron loss, use the formula of Problem~\ref{prob:synchrotron} scaled by $(m_e/m_p)^4$, and compare with the beam energy.

\sol{strings}{10} In kinetic theory, $\eta\sim np\ell$ and $s\sim n\kB$. What does the uncertainty principle say about $p\ell$?

\sol{strings}{11} If the whole system is pure, the radiation's entanglement entropy equals the black hole's. What is the largest entanglement entropy the black hole can have, and how does it change as the black hole evaporates?
